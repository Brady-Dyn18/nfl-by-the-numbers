METHOD + SOURCES

How to read this project

Source

Data comes from the free nflverse releases through nflreadpy. Player statistics provide offense, defense, position, team, opponent, and headshot URLs. Schedules provide game dates and final scores. Team metadata supplies colors and logo URLs.

One row

One row is one player in one regular-season NFL game from 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, or 2025. The five seasons are deliberately limited to completed regular seasons, so the comparisons use the same game type throughout.

Calculations

Win/loss is computed from team score minus opponent score. Win rate is wins divided by team-games. Passing, rushing, receiving, tackles, sacks, and interceptions are summed from player-game records. EPA per attempt is passing EPA divided by pass attempts. Pass share is passing yards divided by passing plus rushing yards.

Limitations

Players without an official statistic in a game are not represented in the player-statistics release. Player totals are not a measure of value by themselves, and these charts show association rather than causation. Images and team marks belong to their respective rights holders and are used for this educational project.
